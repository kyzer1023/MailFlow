MailFlow

Privacy notice

Effective October 26, 2026. MailFlow prepares personalized email campaigns and sends them through the account you connect. This notice covers MailFlow's Microsoft and Google integrations, including staging tests.

Information used

We store your provider's account identifier, name and verified mailbox address to maintain your account. Microsoft and Google accounts remain separate. CSV and XLSX parsing happens in your browser. When you prepare or save work, MailFlow stores message templates, selected recipient addresses, personalization values, message snapshots, attachment metadata, campaign outcomes and operational audit records in Cloudflare D1. Do not upload information you are not authorized to use.

Google and Microsoft permissions

Google sign-in uses your basic profile and email. Gmail's send permission is used only to send messages you authorize through MailFlow, including tests addressed to yourself. MailFlow does not request permission to read your inbox or track opens or clicks. Google Drive app-data permission stores temporary campaign attachments in the connecting account's private appDataFolder, not your ordinary documents. Microsoft uses delegated mailbox sending and OneDrive App Folder permissions. MailFlow does not request application-level Microsoft mailbox permissions or access Google Sheets.

Storage and sharing

Cloudflare hosts the application, database and background queue. Attachments are stored temporarily in the connected account's Google Drive app data or OneDrive App Folder. Message content and selected attachments are sent to your mail provider and the recipients you choose, including CC and BCC. Refresh credentials are encrypted on the server; browser code does not receive them. Sessions use secure cookies on hosted deployments. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. MailFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Campaign records, message snapshots, recipient details and audit evidence currently have no automatic retention purge. Temporary attachments are scheduled for cleanup after eligible terminal campaigns and after unassociated sets expire, normally after 24 hours; unavailable or revoked storage access can delay deletion. Provider recycle bins or backups may retain copies. Accepted and uncertain submission records are retained to prevent duplicate sends. Signing out ends your session but does not delete stored records. Revoking MailFlow in your Google or Microsoft account stops future access after existing access tokens expire, but does not delete stored campaign history or recall sent mail.

Your choices and contact

You can decline optional permissions, disconnect provider access in your account settings, stop pending campaigns, and request account/data deletion through the support email displayed on the MailFlow provider consent screen. Use that private support channel for deletion and privacy requests, not public issue trackers. Include only enough information to identify your account; never send passwords or access tokens. Deletion requests are handled manually, with identity verification and attention to unresolved sending evidence and applicable obligations. There is no automatic deletion deadline promised by this prototype.

Important limits

Provider acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. MailFlow cannot observe mail you send outside the app or guarantee provider quota availability. Google OAuth publishing does not mean Google has verified the app. Unverified consent warnings and Google user or quota limits may apply.
