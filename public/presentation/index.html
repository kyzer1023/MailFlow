Mail Flow: current product and proposed roadmap

Mail Flow

Every send,
accounted for.

Personalized society email with a clear view of progress, recipient results, and the next action.

Sending email needed a clearer view.

Our earlier Power Automate workflow was tedious to prepare and difficult to monitor during real committee work.

Understanding what had happened meant piecing together the automation's progress. We wanted the sending process to be easier to follow and control.

Mail Flow puts the campaign and its recipient results in one place.

This motivation comes from our experience with the previous workflow.

What has happened?
See campaign progress and a processing result for each spreadsheet row.
What needs attention?
Inspect recipient outcomes and keep uncertain submissions visible for review.
What can we do next?
Pause a campaign, resume eligible pending work, or export the results.

A status for each recipient.

See what is pending, what Microsoft accepted, and where to investigate. Paced sending continues after the browser closes.

Accepted by Microsoft confirms submission. It does not confirm inbox delivery. Unknown submissions require review and are not automatically resent.

A spreadsheet becomes individual messages.

Import CSV or Excel, confirm the recipient email column, and check the rows before sending.

One source row creates one separate personalized message when it is eligible to send.

Write once. Personalize each message.

Values from your file

Insert a name, event, date, or another spreadsheet value wherever the message needs it.

Familiar editing

Use visual formatting or sanitized HTML, then review the result for individual recipients.

The details stay under your control.

Your Outlook stays the sender

Set fixed or spreadsheet-based CC, BCC, and Reply-to addresses. Choose message importance.

Campaign attachments

Every recipient gets the same reviewed files. Attach up to five files, totaling 20 MiB, through your OneDrive App Folder.

Attachments are temporary campaign storage. Microsoft mail and OneDrive permissions are required.

Review before the campaign starts.

Check representative personalized messages, send a test to yourself, and acknowledge the final review.

Tests keep the reviewed message and files, while suppressing campaign CC, BCC, and Reply-to.

Useful work stays easy to find.

Reusable flows

A flow saves the message setup. Reuse it with a new recipient file, or edit the template for the next event.

Campaign records

Revisit earlier sends and export recipient outcomes instead of reconstructing what happened.

Ownership today is individual. Shared society access is proposed on the roadmap.

Roadmap / not yet implemented

Clearer preparation and recovery.

The current prototype provides the workflow. These improvements remain planned work before wider use.

  1. 01

    A reviewed version that stays consistent

    Keep draft edits, test sends, and campaign start tied to the same prepared revision, including interrupted requests.

  2. 02

    More predictable reconnection

    Improve recovery when Microsoft authorization expires, preserving unsent work and clearly explaining the next action.

  3. 03

    Deliberate template updates

    Separate one-off message edits from publishing a reusable template. Make missing fields and reuse choices easier to understand.

Roadmap / proposed feature

Society Workspaces.

A shared home for approved members, reusable templates, and committee records.

Approved membership

Admins approve student identities. Verified Microsoft sign-in connects members to the society.

Shared committee memory

Members create and reuse flows, view shared campaigns, and retain records across committee handovers.

Each sender still uses their own mailbox. A permanent society file library needs a separate storage decision.

Roadmap / future options

What could follow.

After preparation and shared access are reliable, committee feedback can guide the next features.

Approval workflows

Let a designated reviewer approve the exact campaign before it starts.

Scheduled sends

Choose when a reviewed campaign begins, with clear ownership and cancellation rules.

Searchable archives

Find campaigns by event or academic year and preserve useful records for the next committee.

The purpose stays the same: clearer visibility and control over society email.

These are proposals, not available features or promised delivery dates.
